### Chapter 1: Is Your Business In Scope?

The EU AI Act applies to any business that:
- Develops AI systems
- Deploys AI systems  
- Distributes or imports AI systems
- Serves EU citizens with AI

If your business uses ChatGPT for customer emails — you're in scope.
If your business uses an AI hiring tool — you're in scope.
If your business has a chatbot on your website — you're in scope.

The question isn't IF the Act applies to you. It's WHICH requirements apply to you, based on risk classification.

**Risk Classification Matrix:**

| Risk Level | Examples | Your Obligations |
|------------|----------|------------------|
| Unacceptable | Social scoring, subliminal manipulation | Banned entirely |
| High | AI in hiring, credit scoring, law enforcement | Full compliance: documentation, oversight, testing |
| Limited | Chatbots, emotion recognition, deepfakes | Transparency: tell users they're interacting with AI |
| Minimal | Spam filters, AI in video games | No specific obligations |

**Quick Self-Assessment:**

Answer these three questions:
1. Does your business use AI that interacts with people? → Limited risk (transparency required)
2. Does your business use AI for hiring, lending, or critical decisions? → High risk (full compliance)
3. Does your business just use AI for internal analytics? → Minimal risk

### Chapter 2: The High-Risk Checklist

If any AI system you use falls into the high-risk category, you need:

**1. Risk Management System**
- Document all identified risks
- Describe mitigation measures
- Update continuously throughout the AI system's lifecycle

**2. Technical Documentation**
- System architecture and design specifications
- Training data and methodology
- Testing results and validation
- Intended purpose and limitations

**3. Record-Keeping**
- Automatic logging of AI system operation
- Event logs stored for minimum periods
- Audit trail for regulatory review

**4. Human Oversight**
- Humans must be able to override AI decisions
- Training required for human overseers
- Monitoring dashboard for AI outputs

**5. Accuracy and Robustness**
- Test against representative datasets
- Document accuracy metrics
- Bias testing across protected characteristics
- Cybersecurity measures (encryption, access controls)

**6. Transparency**
- Inform users when they're interacting with AI
- Label AI-generated content
- Provide meaningful explanations of AI decisions
- Designate a contact point for questions

### Chapter 3: The 90-Day Action Plan

**Days 1-30: Audit**
- Inventory every AI system your business uses
- Classify each by risk level (use Chapter 1 framework)
- Identify compliance gaps for each system
- Assign an internal AI officer (even if part-time)

**Days 31-60: Document**
- Create your risk management framework
- Document technical specifications for each system
- Establish record-keeping procedures
- Draft transparency notices for AI interactions

**Days 61-90: Implement**
- Deploy human oversight mechanisms
- Test AI systems for accuracy and bias
- Train staff on AI Act obligations
- Prepare documentation for regulatory review

### Chapter 4: Irish-Specific Guidance

The Data Protection Commission (DPC) is Ireland's national supervisory authority for the EU AI Act. They work alongside existing GDPR enforcement.

**Key Irish Considerations:**
- DPC has indicated priority enforcement in employment and financial services
- Irish SMEs can leverage Enterprise Ireland digital transformation supports
- AI Act intersects with existing Irish data protection law

**Useful Contacts:**
- Data Protection Commission: dataprotection.ie
- Enterprise Ireland: enterprise-ireland.com
- Local Enterprise Office: localenterprise.ie

### Chapter 5: What Happens If You Don't Comply

Fines under the EU AI Act:
- Up to €35 million or 7% of global turnover for prohibited practices
- Up to €15 million or 3% for most other violations
- Up to €7.5 million or 1.5% for supplying incorrect information

Beyond fines: reputational damage, loss of business partners, exclusion from public contracts, and potential criminal liability for directors.
