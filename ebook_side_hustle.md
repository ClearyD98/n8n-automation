# The 5-Hour Side Hustle
## How to Build a Second Income Stream While Working Full-Time

**By D.C. Cleary**

---

### Introduction

You don't need to quit your job to start making money on the side. You don't need funding. You don't need a business plan. You need five hours a week and a willingness to start before you feel ready.

I built my first side income while working 9-6 in enterprise operations. Five hours a week — evenings, weekends, the odd lunch break. Within six months, I had multiple income streams operating without my constant attention.

This book is the playbook. What to build. How to price it. Where to find customers. How to make it run without you.

---

### Chapter 1: Pick Your Play

**Service-Based (fastest start, €50-500/client)**
- Consulting in whatever you already do professionally
- Freelancing on Upwork/Fiverr
- Tutoring or coaching
- Virtual assistant work

**Product-Based (slower start, passive once built)**
- Ebooks and digital guides
- Templates and toolkits
- Online courses
- Print-on-demand merchandise

**Platform-Based (middle ground)**
- Affiliate marketing
- Content creation (YouTube, Medium, Substack)
- App and software development
- Dropshipping and ecommerce

The best side hustle is the one you'll actually do. Pick based on your skills, not what's trending.

---

### Chapter 2: The 5-Hour Weekly System

**Hour 1: Build.** Create your product. Write your ebook. Record your course. Design your template.

**Hour 2: Market.** Post on social media. Answer questions in forums. Reach out to potential clients. Tell people you exist.

**Hour 3: Sell.** Follow up on leads. Send proposals. Close deals. Process payments.

**Hour 4: Deliver.** Do the work. Ship the product. Respond to customers.

**Hour 5: Improve.** What worked this week? What didn't? Fix one thing. Automate one thing. Document one process.

---

### Chapter 3: Pricing That Works

Most people underprice because they're afraid of rejection. Here's what actually works:

- If nobody says it's too expensive, you're charging too little.
- Double your first instinct. Then add 20%.
- Price by value, not by time. A €500 template that saves someone 50 hours is cheap.
- Offer three tiers. Most people pick the middle one.

---

### Chapter 4: Finding Customers

**Free Methods:**
- Answer questions on Reddit, Quora, and industry forums
- Post valuable content on LinkedIn and Twitter/X
- Join Facebook groups where your customers hang out
- Ask existing contacts for referrals

**Paid Methods:**
- Targeted ads on the platform where your customers are
- Sponsor newsletters in your niche
- Pay for directory listings

Start free. Only spend money on what's already working.

---

### Chapter 5: Making It Run Without You

The goal isn't a second job. It's a second income that doesn't need your constant attention.

- Document every process
- Automate everything repetitive
- Create templates for common tasks
- Build a waitlist so you're never starting from zero

When your side hustle can run for a week without you touching it, you've built an asset, not a job.
