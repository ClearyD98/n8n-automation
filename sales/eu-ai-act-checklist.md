## Prepared by Dylan Cleary, autoprod.io

## Part 1: Are You In Scope?

### Risk Classification Matrix

| Risk Level | Description | Your Obligation |
|-----------|-------------|-----------------|
| **Minimal** | AI with no impact on rights/safety (spam filters, basic analytics) | Transparency only |
| **Limited** | AI that interacts with humans (chatbots, emotion recognition) | Transparency + user notification |
| **High** | AI used in critical infrastructure, employment, education, law enforcement | Full compliance: documentation, oversight, accuracy, cybersecurity |

### Quick Decision Tree

- Does your AI interact with EU citizens? → YES: Continue
- Is it a chatbot or generates content? → YES: Limited Risk (transparency obligations)
- Does it impact hiring, lending, law enforcement, or critical infrastructure? → YES: High Risk (full compliance)
- Is it a basic tool (spam filter, analytics)? → YES: Minimal Risk

## Part 2: High-Risk AI Systems Checklist

### Mandatory Documentation

[ ] Risk management system established and documented
[ ] Technical documentation maintained (design specs, training data, testing)
[ ] Record-keeping of AI system operation (logging enabled)
[ ] Instructions for use provided to deployers

### Human Oversight

[ ] Human review capability built into AI decision pipeline
[ ] Override mechanism available for operators
[ ] Training provided to human overseers
[ ] Monitoring dashboard for AI decisions

### Accuracy and Robustness

[ ] Testing performed against representative datasets
[ ] Accuracy metrics documented and within acceptable thresholds
[ ] Bias testing completed (protected characteristics)
[ ] Cybersecurity measures implemented (encryption, access controls)

### Transparency

[ ] Users notified when interacting with AI
[ ] AI-generated content labeled as such
[ ] Explanation capability for AI decisions
[ ] Contact point for questions about AI decisions

### Conformity Assessment

[ ] Self-assessment completed against EU AI Act requirements
[ ] Documentation package prepared for regulatory review
[ ] Post-market monitoring plan established
[ ] Serious incident reporting procedure in place

## Part 4: Your 90-Day Action Plan

### Week 1-2: Audit
- Inventory all AI systems in use
- Classify each by risk level
- Identify compliance gaps

### Week 3-4: Documentation
- Create risk management framework
- Document technical specifications
- Establish record-keeping procedures

### Week 5-8: Implementation
- Deploy human oversight mechanisms
- Implement transparency measures
- Test for accuracy and bias

### Week 9-12: Review
- Conduct conformity self-assessment
- Prepare for regulatory review
- Train staff on AI Act obligations

## Irish-Specific Notes

The Irish Data Protection Commission (DPC) is the national supervisory authority for the EU AI Act in Ireland. They work alongside the existing GDPR enforcement framework.

**Contact:** dataprotection.ie

**Key Irish considerations:**
- DPC has indicated it will prioritize high-risk AI in employment and financial services
- Irish SMEs should leverage Enterprise Ireland's digital transformation supports
- The AI Act intersects with existing Irish data protection law — GDPR compliance is foundational
