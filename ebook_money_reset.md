# The 30-Day Money Reset
## A No-Bullshit Guide to Taking Control of Your Finances

**By D.C. Cleary**

---

### Introduction

Most personal finance books are written by people who've never been broke. They tell you to skip lattes and invest in index funds while you're trying to figure out how to pay rent.

This isn't that book.

I wrote this because I needed it. When I was staring at €23,000 in debt with no clear way out, the standard advice wasn't helpful. "Cut back on discretionary spending" means nothing when you've already cut everything.

What worked wasn't one big change. It was a system — a 30-day reset that changed how I thought about money, not just how I spent it. This book is that system.

No judgment. No lectures. Just what works.

---

### Week 1: Face the Numbers

**Day 1: The Full Inventory**
Write down every debt. Every balance. Every interest rate. Every minimum payment. Don't judge. Just record.

**Day 2: The Real Budget**
Not what you think you spend. What you actually spend. Go through 3 months of bank statements. Categorize everything. Most people are off by 30-50%.

**Day 3: The Leak Finder**
Find the subscriptions you forgot about. The auto-renewals. The "just this once" that became weekly. Cancel everything you can.

**Day 4: The Income Map**
List every source of money. Salary. Side work. Gifts. Tax refunds. Everything. Then list 5 ways you could add €100/month.

**Day 5: The Emergency Question**
If you lost all income tomorrow, how long could you survive? This number is uncomfortable. Good. It's supposed to be.

**Day 6: The Negotiation Day**
Call every provider. Internet. Phone. Insurance. Say "I'm reviewing my budget and need to reduce costs. What can you do?" Most will offer a discount rather than lose you.

**Day 7: Rest. Review. Reset.**
Look at what you've found. Pick the 3 biggest money leaks. Those are your Week 2 targets.

---

### Week 2: Stop the Bleeding

The money you don't spend is worth more than the money you earn. It's tax-free. It requires zero effort. And it's immediately available.

This week is about elimination, not addition. No new income streams. No side hustles. Just stopping the money from leaving.

---

### Week 3: Build the Engine

Now we flip from defense to offense.

**Day 15: The First €100**
Find one way to make €100 this week. Sell something. Do a gig. Offer a skill. The amount doesn't matter — the proof does. You can generate money when you need to.

**Day 16-21: Systematize**
Whatever made that first €100 — can you do it again? Weekly? Automate it. Document it. Make it repeatable.

---

### Week 4: Protect and Grow

**Day 22-28: The Buffer**
Your goal by Day 30: one month of expenses in a separate account. Not investments. Not crypto. Cash. Accessible. This is your "I quit my job" money, your "car broke down" money, your "I need options" money.

**Day 29: The 6-Month Plan**
Where do you want to be in 6 months? Pick ONE financial goal. Not five. One. Debt paid off. Emergency fund built. Income stream established. Pick and commit.

**Day 30: The System**
Money management isn't about willpower. It's about systems. Automate what you can. Review weekly. Adjust monthly. The goal isn't perfection — it's progress.

---

### The Rules

1. Spend less than you earn. Everything else is details.
2. Debt is a mathematical problem, not a moral one.
3. The best investment is the one you actually make.
4. Negotiate everything. The worst they can say is no.
5. Money in the bank is freedom. Everything else is speculation.
