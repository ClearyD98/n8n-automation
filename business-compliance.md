## Step 1: Register as a Sole Trader (Immediate — This Week)

### What You Need To Do:
1. **Register with Revenue for income tax** — File Form TR1 (Tax Registration) on Revenue Online Service (ROS)
   - Register for: Income Tax, Preliminary Tax, USC, PRSI
   - Link: https://www.ros.ie
   - Your PPS number is your tax reference number
   
2. **Register a business name** (optional but recommended for "autoprod")
   - Register "autoprod" as a business name with CRO (Companies Registration Office)
   - Cost: €20 (online) or €40 (paper)
   - Link: https://www.cro.ie
   - This lets you trade as "autoprod" officially (not required for sole trader using your own name)

3. **VAT registration** — NOT required until revenue exceeds €40,000 (services) or €80,000 (goods) in a 12-month period
   - You do NOT need to register for VAT yet
   - When revenue approaches €40K, register voluntarily to reclaim VAT on business expenses

### Tax Obligations as Sole Trader:
| Tax | Rate | When |
|-----|------|------|
| Income Tax | 20% up to €42,000; 40% above | Annual return by 31 October (paper) or mid-November (online) |
| USC | 0.5% to 11% (progressive) | Same as income tax |
| PRSI | 4% on all income | Same as income tax |
| Preliminary Tax | 100% of prior year or 90% of current year estimate | 31 October each year |

### Example Tax Calculation (€60,000 annual profit):
- Income Tax: €8,400 (20% band) + €7,200 (40% on remainder) = €15,600
- USC: ~€2,100
- PRSI: €2,400
- **Total tax: ~€20,100 (33.5% effective rate)**
- **Keep 35% of all revenue in a separate account for taxes**

## Step 3: Insurance (Before First Client)

### Required:
- **Professional Indemnity Insurance** — Covers you if a client claims your advice caused them financial loss
  - AI governance consulting carries risk: if your framework is found non-compliant, client could sue
  - Minimum €250,000 cover recommended (standard for consultants)
  - Cost: ~€400-800/year
  - Providers: Hiscox Ireland, Allianz Ireland, Zurich Ireland, or specialist brokers

### Recommended:
- **Public Liability Insurance** — If you ever visit client offices
  - Cost: ~€200-400/year
- **Cyber Insurance** — Covers data breaches if you handle client data
  - Cost: ~€300-600/year

### Key Point: Include a LIMITATION OF LIABILITY clause in every contract
"Consultant's total liability under this agreement shall not exceed the fees paid by Client for the services giving rise to the claim."

## Step 4: Client Contract Template (Before First Client)

### Every engagement needs a written agreement covering:
1. **Scope of work** — What exactly are you delivering?
2. **Fees and payment terms** — €2,500/5,000, payable 50% upfront, 50% on delivery
3. **Limitation of liability** — Caps your exposure to fees paid
4. **Intellectual property** — You retain methodology IP; client gets deliverables for internal use
5. **Confidentiality** — Both parties protect each other's information
6. **Governing law** — Republic of Ireland
7. **Dispute resolution** — Mediation first, then Irish courts

### Payment Terms (Recommended):
- 50% upfront (non-refundable deposit) — covers your time and commitment
- 50% on delivery of final report
- Net-14 payment terms (client pays within 14 days of invoice)

## Step 5: Digital Infrastructure Compliance

### GDPR (You're Processing Client Data):
- You need a Privacy Policy on autoprod.io (required if you collect any data — even email addresses)
- You need a Cookie Consent banner (the landing page form + any analytics)
- You need to document what data you process, why, and for how long
- If you use third-party tools (email, forms, analytics), list them in Privacy Policy
- **Register with DPC as a data controller** (required if processing personal data commercially)

### ePrivacy / Cookie Law:
- Implied consent required for non-essential cookies
- The landing page should have a cookie banner

### Consumer Rights Directive (if selling to consumers):
- 14-day cooling-off period for digital products
- Digital products exempt IF consumer explicitly waives right + acknowledges loss of withdrawal right

**Bottom line:** You're operating as an unregistered sole trader with no insurance, no contracts, and no privacy policy. The biggest risk right now is personal liability — if a client sues, your personal assets (savings, property, etc.) are exposed.
