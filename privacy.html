Privacy policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026

This policy explains what personal data autoprod ("we") collects through autoprod.io and how we use it. Questions or requests: hello@autoprod.io.

What we collect

  • Purchase data: when you pay, Stripe collects your name, email, billing details and payment card. We receive your name, email, billing country, what you bought and the amount. We never see your full card number.
  • Emails you send us, and the information you share for a session (for example, an overview of your business and the systems you use).
  • Analytics: this site uses Google Analytics, which sets cookies and records pages visited, approximate location, device and browser, so we can see how the site is used.

Why we use it, and the legal basis

  • To take payment, deliver what you bought and contact you about it: performance of a contract.
  • To keep accounting and tax records: legal obligation.
  • To understand site usage through analytics: legitimate interests, or your consent where that is required.

Who we share it with

Stripe (payments), Google (analytics), and our email and hosting providers (GitHub Pages hosts this site). Some of these providers process data outside the EEA under the European Commission's standard contractual clauses or an adequacy decision. We do not sell your personal data.

How long we keep it

We keep purchase and accounting records for six years, as Irish tax law requires. We keep session materials for as long as we are working with you and for up to two years after, unless you ask us to delete them sooner.

Your rights

You can ask for access to, correction of, deletion of or a copy of your data, and you can object to or restrict how we use it. Email hello@autoprod.io. You can also complain to the Data Protection Commission in Ireland (dataprotection.ie).

Cookies

The only cookies this site sets are Google Analytics cookies. You can block or delete them in your browser settings at any time.
